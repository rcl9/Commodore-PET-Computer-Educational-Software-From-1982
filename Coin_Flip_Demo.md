## Coin Flip Demo

This PET program can be used with grade 9 and 10 students with 'Statistics and an Introduction to Probability'.

It can also be used as a demonstration of probability in grade 13. It can simulate up to 20,000 coin tosses, 
tabulates the results and display the results on a chart and on three different graphs.
